Project Brief

The task was to design a multi-page website in Figma based on a self-chosen topic. I created an immersive travel concept documenting a journey through Australia, focusing on visual storytelling, layout, and user experience.

01
Research & Moodboarding
Collected visual references, studied existing travel sites and defined a distinctive colour palette inspired by Australian landscapes.
02
Wireframing in Figma
Sketched and iterated on information architecture across all pages — deciding on a scrolling narrative structure that guides the visitor through the journey.
03
Design & Prototype
Built a high-fidelity prototype in Figma with interactive flows, hover states and transitions.
